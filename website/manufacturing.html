From Cell to Shipped Pack: Our Process

BOM-to-process quality control at every step.

STEP 1 · INCOMING

Cell Inspection

100% capacity, IR, and dimension check on every incoming cell batch.

STEP 2 · ASSEMBLY

Pack Assembly

Laser welding, BMS integration, harness routing under clean-room conditions.

STEP 3 · AGING

72h Aging Test

Every pack cycles through full charge-discharge to flush infant mortality.

STEP 4 · QC

EOL Verification

Capacity, IR, BMS calibration, insulation, hi-pot. Statistical sampling for lot release.

STEP 5 · SHIP

Export Packing

UN-rated packaging, MSDS, COO documentation. Sea, air, courier as required.
