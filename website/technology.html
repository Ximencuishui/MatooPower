Safety First

LFP Battery · The Safer Chemistry

All Matoo Power products use automotive-grade lithium iron phosphate (LFP) cells. LFP is the safest mainstream battery chemistry — significantly more stable than NMC or NCA alternatives under stress conditions.

  • ✓ Needle penetration test: No fire, no explosion
  • ✓ Crush test: No fire, no explosion
  • ✓ Overcharge test: No fire, no explosion
  • ✓ Thermal stability: Up to 60°C operating range
  • ✓ Cycle life: 2000+ cycles at 80% depth of discharge
